# Burndown Planning

The spreadsheet `burndown-chart-data.xlsx` contains realistic daily burndown data for four sprints:

| Sprint | Scope | Total Story Points | Sprint Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint 1 | Frontend Completion | 12 | 10 working days |
| Sprint 2 | Backend APIs | 36 | 10 working days |
| Sprint 3 | Database Integration | 32 | 10 working days |
| Sprint 4 | Testing + Docker + Deployment | 25 | 10 working days |

Each row includes sprint name, day, ideal remaining points, actual remaining points, completed points, and notes. The data intentionally includes realistic non-linear progress: discovery days, mid-sprint acceleration, and final stabilization.
